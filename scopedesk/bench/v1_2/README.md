This README is the single current contract for v1.2. v1 and v1.1 remain in git history (v1 files at `scopedesk/bench/`, v1.1 at `scopedesk/bench/v1_1/`) and must not be used for evaluation. ## What it is
131 natural-language requests in 8 families. Each request ends with its declared conventions and answer vocabulary (`conventions.json`, hashed). Verdict ∈ `applies | out_of_scope | underdetermined`, defined **per request**: *applies* = the cited statement's conclusion for the question asked equals the exact conclusion under the stated conventions; *out_of_scope* = it does not; *underdetermined* = a needed input or convention is not given and can change the answer. Scope conditions that are violated without changing the conclusion are recorded in the key's `violated_conditions` field (diagnosis), not in the verdict. Items are declared-model checks: they do not establish anything about tissues or organisms.

| family | cited statement | what the key computes (exact unless noted) |
|---|---|---|
| turing_finite_domain (22) | Turing diffusion-driven instability (Delphi `turing-ddi`) | exists a linearly unstable admissible mode on the stated 1-D domain; Neumann/Dirichlet q=(nπ/L)², periodic ring of circumference L q=(2nπ/L)²; unstated boundary ⇒ underdetermined if verdicts differ |
| turing_2d_rectangle (15) | same, on a rectangle | Neumann q=(m/a)²+(n/b)², periodic torus q=(2m/a)²+(2n/b)², (m,n)≠(0,0) |

The rayleigh_plateau family of v1 was removed (an inadmissible mode made its scope condition wrong). Four items were excluded after blinded audits (three false-premise Turing items whose verdict depends on whether 'does the condition give the right answer' means existence of a growing mode; one wavelength item whose verdict flips with the reading of 'fastest-growing'), plus one wavelength near-tie; every exclusion is in `excluded.json` with its reason (legacy generator ids; the private map links them to opaque ids).

## Files
`instances_public.jsonl` (metadata + request text), **`model_payload.jsonl` (the exact text a model receives: id + prompt, nothing else; hashed)**, `instances_full.jsonl` (+hidden parameters; never shown), `key.jsonl`, `excluded.json`, `split.json`, `conventions.json`, `id_map_PRIVATE.json` (legacy generator id → opaque public id; **not** model-visible), `KEY.sha256` (hashes of all of these and the generator sources). Public ids are `q` + 8 hex characters from a hash of (seed, legacy id): independent of generation order and of the label; file order is a seeded shuffle.

## Split and grouping
Dev (prompt/harness tuning allowed): `turing_finite_domain`, `hawk_dove_ess`. Held out: the other six. **The 1-D Turing, 2-D Turing and wavelength families share the same Jacobian/mode mathematics** (`split.json: mechanism_groups`): report both the 8-family analysis and the 6-group analysis with these three merged, and describe held-out Turing-2D/wavelength results as within-mechanism transfer, not unseen-mechanism generalisation. Item-level binomial intervals are descriptive only (items within a family are not independent).

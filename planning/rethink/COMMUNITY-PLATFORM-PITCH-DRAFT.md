# Delphi community platform: proposed product and pitch

Requested discussion draft, October 31, 2026. This is a proposed direction, not a built or deployed community platform. The broader autonomous goal remains paused. Existing director/host own selection and integration; no new agents are launched.

## The vision

**Delphi turns biological knowledge into reusable components that carry their evidence and assumptions.** Scientists and agents contribute candidate claims, mathematical models, formal derivations, empirical tests and counterexamples. The shared library helps a researcher decide what applies to a particular design and what still needs to be measured.

The useful community unit is a **scoped result**, with original source/version; exact proposition/model; applicability assumptions and units; proof or executable test; measured/predicted/simulated distinctions; counterexamples; and versioned dependencies. Its page separately reports source fidelity, kernel checking, numerical convergence, empirical support and unresolved questions. An empirical claim can have a formalized test without being settled by a theorem. There is no universal “verified biology” badge.

## Community workflow

1. A scientist submits a concrete question or candidate claim with its source, such as whether a specified model permits a pattern on a stated finite domain.
2. Contributors expose missing assumptions and formulate an exact target. Model authors and statement reviewers are identified separately.
3. Contributors submit proofs, executable scope checks, independent simulations, data-linked tests or distinguishing counterexamples. Machine-checkable obligations run in bounded CI; source correspondence still needs review.
4. Reviewed versions become reusable Python/MCP/library components and citable evidence records. Reuse links preserve dependencies; changed sources/assumptions trigger rechecking or refusal.
5. New requests exercise the modules. Maintainers publish corrections and unresolved results alongside successful applications.

A readable derivation graph connects known mathematical roots to scoped results. Price → regression-form Hamilton is now a genuine compiled proof dependency in our prototype. Fisher's additive-genetic theorem is still pending. The biological derivation is established literature, credited as such.

## Why people would join: hypotheses to test

- Researchers obtain an inspectable scope check or test for a claim they already need, plus attribution and a citable artifact.
- Formalizers work on bounded missions with frozen target statements, test cases and visible scientific uses.
- Model builders obtain reusable mathematical checks and counterexamples before running expensive simulations or experiments.
- Experimentalists supply measurements or discriminatory tests that address explicit gaps; they receive credit for empirical support and refutation separately from formal work.
- Companies can contribute general model contracts or public benchmark tasks while retaining private raw data. Private evidence must remain visibly unavailable to public reviewers; it cannot silently create a public validation claim.

Measure contribution value by downstream reuse, independently detected errors, time to a justified decision and resolved explicit obligations. Theorem counts and model votes alone reward volume and can hide weakened statements. Reputation should name the contribution type and review history. Begin with attribution; payment/bounties are a later product hypothesis, not a requirement.

## First community release

Use a public repository and static mission/result pages, issue/PR review, pinned Lean/Mathlib CI and executable Python/MCP scope checks. Prepare citable nanopublication drafts; actual signing/publication requires the established authorization and receipts. Adapt existing proof-collaboration infrastructure where useful: [Prove2Me](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.28433v2) already supplies the collaborative formalization precedent. Credit formal-biology predecessors too.

Launch a small, curated set of real requests with named maintainers and explicit acceptance tests. Lead with two connected proved nodes and one end-to-end model decision, rather than 880 extracted claims presented as resolved. Retain original TrpB null results and do not claim collective superiority before a matched evaluation. Community adoption requires actual contributors/use, not internal self-tests or outreach drafts.

## Three-minute pitch spine

**Problem:** AI can propose a biological design while relying on a model whose assumptions do not hold for that design. A citation or a plausible explanation does not expose that failure automatically.

**Product:** Delphi is a community-maintained library of biological-model results with executable assumptions, evidence and checks. Each new verified component can be reused by people and agents.

**Demonstration:** Show a concrete pattern-design request, the omitted finite-domain condition, a rejected model decision and a checked repair. Label linear mathematics, nonlinear simulation and observed biology separately. Then open the derivation graph: finite Price identity → conditional Hamilton criterion, proof source and rejected sign mutant.

**Evidence available now:** Price and regression-form Hamilton compiled with standard axioms; two changed-target sign mutants rejected and four explicit witness/control theorems compiled. An independent audit caught a volume-violating mode in our own proposed liquid-bridge benchmark before agent evaluation. These are mathematical/software results, not measured biological validation or a measured collective advantage.

**Expansion:** Turn isolated literature claims into a growing library of reusable reasoning components for model-based biological design. Test the value with external researchers bringing concrete requests and measuring whether the library changes a justified decision.

Possible closing line: **“Every contribution should leave biology easier to reason about—and leave the assumptions where the next person can check them.”**

The pitch still needs a polished live decision demo, independent statement review, corrected benchmark evaluation and actual community use. A credible ambitious vision exists; a winning outcome is not established.

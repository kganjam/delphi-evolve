## Slide approval rule

Every factual sentence needs an ID above or a newly reviewed row, exact source artifact/revision, evidence kind and check. Use “under the declared model” with model mathematics; use “simulated” with PDE outcomes; use “observed” only with measurements. Cite known mathematics as known. Market/company/pilot statements must be framed as a proposal unless a user/customer or prospective result is actually documented. Remove unsupported universals and priority claims. A compelling frame available now is: **“Make the assumptions behind biological-model decisions executable and auditable.”**

The agent-gate corrections are binding: a linear-mode verdict is not a pattern-formation verdict; a zero-answer policy has zero coverage and undefined selective risk; a kinetics-specific robustness sweep is not a universal transport threshold. Correct `underdetermined`/`out_of_scope` diagnoses are substantive answers, while abstention and parser failure must be recorded separately. The prior host-reported 24/24 agreements on prompts mismatched to their labels cannot establish valid predictive accuracy or a frontier-model ceiling. Full pre-run review and repair requirements are pinned at 9daeb11.

This ledger is a review gate, not a claim that the project is complete. Missing results stay pending; numbers change only when new receipts justify them.

The user's open-problem/collective-coordination vision is developed in `ACTIVE-FRONTIER-PITCH-PROPOSAL.md`. Its problem pages, frontier allocation, federation and community execution loop are proposals. Current compiled probes and walkthrough receipts can illustrate the first step; they do not establish a deployed autonomous research network, a field-level open problem solved, a new general architecture, or measured collective advantage.

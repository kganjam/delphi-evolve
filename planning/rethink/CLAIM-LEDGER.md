## Slide approval rule

Every factual sentence needs an ID above or a newly reviewed row, exact source artifact/revision, evidence kind and check. Use “under the declared model” with model mathematics; use “simulated” with PDE outcomes; use “observed” only with measurements. Cite known mathematics as known. Market/company/pilot statements must be framed as a proposal unless a user/customer or prospective result is actually documented. Remove unsupported universals and priority claims. A compelling frame available now is: **“Make the assumptions behind biological-model decisions executable and auditable.”**

This ledger is a review gate, not a claim that the project is complete. Missing results stay pending; numbers change only when new receipts justify them.

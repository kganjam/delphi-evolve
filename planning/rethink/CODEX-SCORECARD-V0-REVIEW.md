A 10% benchmark tolerance is not a universal validity boundary. |

Our compiled conditional corollary is useful evidence; no a/b choice should imply a discovered defect in this record or a complete empirical test. |
| `maynardsmith-hawk-dove` | The matrix and C>V are present in the record. The checked algebra concerns the symmetric two-strategy model. ## Counting and receipt boundaries

Use: **“21 extracted records: 6 linked to scope-check benchmark families, 1 with a related formal lemma but its target unestablished, 2 requiring empirical/framework treatment, 12 without a corresponding check.”** If retaining “7 attempted,” define it as seven artifact-linked records and disclose that one lacks a faithful target check. Prefer the explicit breakdown.

Do not collapse five `a` rows into “five false biological laws,” “five omitted assumptions,” or “five Delphi errors.” Four already name material scope conditions in their mathematics/context. A record can have a correct theorem, a misleading headline, and an incomplete operational specification; keep these as separate judgments. Source faithfulness to the original papers remains unreviewed.

`scripts/build_frontier_review_map.py` independently checks all 21 field hashes, copied headline/math/source/context, unique IDs and original status counts against the pinned critic files. It produces a record-level receipt and a map data export. These checks establish transcription/count integrity, not theorem validity. Prior benchmark/Lean receipts remain separate and are not rerun or upgraded by this review.

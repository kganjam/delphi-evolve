# Preliminary statement-fidelity review of the 21-claim scorecard

Codex API, October 31, 2026. Reviewed available **three-claim Turing draft only**, `artifacts/claims_spec/turing_scorecard.json` at collab source `05be25f0d8b478bb1fd40f52cdecb888c85a8bab`. The complete 21-record freeze/target set has not yet been supplied to this reviewer. This is not approval of that set and not a new formalization result. The director and collab owner received these findings before this memo.

1. **Fix the formal target before proving.** The DDI representation says instability iff `S>2 sqrt(Du Dv det J)` “at an ADMISSIBLE q”. This inequality has no q in it. Under stable well-mixed kinetics and positive diffusivities, the finite-domain target is instead `exists admissible q: det(J-qD)<0`. Equivalently the admissible q must lie strictly inside the open unstable band. Non-empty continuum band plus existence of some admissible q is insufficient. The original pi-domain example is already a counterexample to that mistranslation.
2. **Onset is not an attained minimum.** For the original J,D and Neumann boundary, `sqrt(2)*pi` is the infimum of domain lengths admitting strict linear instability; the first mode is marginal at equality. Use a strict inequality and the verified bracket. The admissibility verdict is also non-monotone at larger lengths. Do not call this a minimum length guaranteeing a nonlinear pattern.
3. **Scope conditions are restored to the extracted record, not discovered in Turing's theory.** Quote the record and original source separately. Finite-domain eigenmodes were already part of the established literature. “We had to supply” must mean “our extracted claim or request omitted it.” It does not establish a textbook or original-author error.
4. **Do not equate empirical with unformalizable.** Replace “empirical and not formalizable” by “empirical; not settled by this model theorem” or “not a theorem target in this evaluation”. Empirical hypotheses can have mathematically formalized operational tests; a mechanistic framework may admit multiple mathematical models. “No canonical formalization” in an extracted record does not prove none exists.
5. **The wavelength headline contradicts its own caveat.** “Spacing is set by chemistry, not tissue size” needs near-onset/large-domain qualifications and must distinguish an intrinsic continuum scale from discrete admissible linear modes and nonlinear observed patterns. Its draft correctly reports no compiled wavelength theorem: retain that status.
6. **Count proved statements, not resolved biological laws.** One existing finite-domain classification/tail package does not formalize the entire continuum theorem, prove its PDE correspondence, or establish the natural-language biological claim. Report record coverage, accepted formal statements, checked instances, source-fidelity review and empirical residuals separately. An unresolved record does not need a pretend proof/mutant/axiom receipt.
7. **Freeze source and meaning.** The final inventory needs exact local/network source bytes and hashes, definition/import/toolchain hashes, each full formal target, its relation to source text, preconditions and exclusions, expected axiom policy and meaningful counterexamples/mutants. Hash the final version before proving. Any late repair is versioned/disclosed; an old receipt is identified as reuse rather than a new successful run.

A useful two-axis record format is:

- **Source proposition status:** accurately transcribed / missing assumptions / ambiguous / empirical question / unresolved correspondence.
- **Evidence status:** formal target frozen / kernel checked with axiom audit / numerical check only / observed test / no check; plus the exact proposition actually checked.

This avoids forcing “conditional model lemma proved” into “biological law holds”. The original four buckets can remain a high-level display if their definitions preserve these distinctions and the underlying fields carry the residual uncertainty.

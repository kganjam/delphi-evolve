Read the common handoff, user requests, original 20→10→3→1 plan and research, but make an independent judgment. The user prioritizes the idea/pitch above feature count and has not accepted the previous negative-result systems prototype as a winning entry.

- [ ] Recheck the official judging rubric, schedule and sponsor rules. Distinguish listed points from unverified event guidance; do not invent a score or probability of winning.
- [ ] Generate at least ten fresh or sharply reframed ideas, including one outside TrpB/NCA/bioelectric. Narrow to three with a written reason for each rejection. A prior survey already exists; contribute new judgment grounded in reachable validation.
- [ ] Check FutureHouse problems and concrete evaluation opportunities, but distinguish threshold auditing from solving a biological problem. Compare closely with Anthropic/Adaptyv, modern inverse-design and mechanobiology work.
- [ ] For top three: one-sentence pitch, the 20-second visual reveal, actual scientific question, existing-data availability, strongest baseline, what Delphi changes, what multiple agents add, what Lean could prove non-vacuously, and a real acceptance criterion for today.

- [ ] State a buyer/use case and a plausible next paid pilot without fabricating market size, customers or savings. Explain why the current product might not be needed.

- [ ] After selection, red-team the implementation's headline claim and help rewrite the final pitch around actual outcomes. Take a follow-up `/goal` from the director.

Do not let "scientific honesty" become the entire novelty claim. It is a requirement; the product must accomplish something worth checking.

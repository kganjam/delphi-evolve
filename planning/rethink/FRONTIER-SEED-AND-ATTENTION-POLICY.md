# Seed the frontier with work that can actually move

2026-10-31, Seattle PDT. `site/review/frontier-problems.json` contains six unresolved project questions and one completed scope correction from existing work. Each has a question, known state, acceptance criterion, next action, required capabilities, downstream uses, dependencies, cached assets and source. None is advertised as a new field-wide unsolved problem.

F001: faithful Fisher formalization; F002: molecular-to-tissue evidence bridge; F003: linear versus nonlinear simulation outcomes; F004: source-to-claim fidelity; F005: independent reuse; F006: attention/allocation evaluation. F007 preserves the resolved volume-constraint correction as a reusable result. Do not convert every unchecked record or agent-generated question into a scientific frontier claim.

The six open seeds were created as [issues 1–6 in kganjam/delphi-frontier](https://github.com/kganjam/delphi-frontier/issues). The repository remains private; collaborators need access. Issue creation is a real coordination action, not evidence of participation or scientific progress. Exact links are in the seed JSON; the resolved correction is retained in the map without manufacturing a newly completed issue.

## Interest, work and resources are different signals

Register four separate, retractable, versioned signals:

* **Following / interested:** “I want updates or consider this important.”
* **Can contribute:** a named capability, availability and proposed bounded task.
* **Can provide resources:** a resource offer with quantity, units, restrictions and expiry.
* **Blocked by this:** a link to a concrete downstream problem/action and an explanation of what resolving this would enable.

Allow endorsement with a reason, counterargument and evidence. Never merge these into a displayed probability of truth. Identities, organizations and shared model/source ancestry matter when estimating independence; many accounts or model calls do not create independent support. Record a human operator where applicable and make self-declared versus verified capability visible.

The first coordination surface can be repository issues: one seed per issue, comments with a structured contribution or interest record, and reactions as a lightweight attention signal. Issues are coordination artifacts, not a scientific acceptance engine or a measured voting market. Do not infer a reservation, commitment or scientific review from a reaction.

## Start with a small, contestable allocation policy

Use an explicit curator queue while collecting signals. Surface separate views: newly actionable, blocking downstream work, seeking independent review, neglected but important, and exploratory. Let people choose the lens rather than hiding everything in one ranking number. Display why an item appears and who set its value assumptions.

A later bounded budget can let each verified participant allocate a limited number of nontransferable attention credits across questions, with declared caps and a separate review/exploration allocation. This expresses preferences, not objective scientific value. Quadratic voting/funding, auctions, prediction markets or retroactive rewards are candidates for experiments; they are not automatically robust to correlated actors, Sybils or unverifiable outcomes. No money or token mechanism is implemented by this proposal.

Reward completed, accepted work and observed useful reuse; recognize negative results, corrections, curation and independent replication. Require meaningful acceptance predicates before posting bounties. Reserve funding for long-horizon or difficult-to-score work rather than optimizing entirely for short easy tasks. Compare any mechanism to expert prioritization and simple round-robin/value-cost baselines.

## From a discussion thread to an action graph

The graph should retain a versioned chain: question → criterion → prerequisite → proposed action → reservation → run → artifact → review → accepted result or dispute → downstream action. A post can propose any of these; it does not silently advance their states. A result can resolve one narrow question while leaving its parent question open.

Before scheduling a run, search for reusable artifacts. The cache key includes problem/target version, input hashes, code/model/environment versions, parameters, scope and acceptance-policy version. Verify access, provenance, integrity and current review state. A scientifically incompatible cached result is a lead, not a cache hit. Superseded premises invalidate applicability even when the artifact bytes remain identical. Record both successful reuse and rejected transfer, including why.

Agentic attention routes a specific change to the people/actions whose decisions it may affect. Agentic engineering produces or repairs the artifact. Epistemic review decides exactly what the artifact establishes. Resource allocation funds the next useful step. Keep these roles distinguishable and link their receipts so humans can read what happened.

The product goes beyond a feed when a reader can see **what changed, why it is credible within scope, who can use it, and what action becomes possible**. An attractive map and community discussion help navigation; demonstrable action and reusable evidence are the outcome.

Scope. one two-species linear family, ensembles E1/E2 and the noise definition chosen by us; absolute values move by about 10 to 20 points with the noise model; not a property of any organism. This is not a universal robustness cliff and not a property of any organism. It has not been through the claim ledger and its prior art is unchecked. A literature ratio for Nodal and Lefty is not plotted: the source numbers have not been read directly in this tree, and a literature point could only be descriptive context. A diffusion-ratio threshold of 10 as a universal rule is false: a linear counterexample uses 1.1.

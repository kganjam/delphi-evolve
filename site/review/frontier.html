      01 / Challenge

      “Show me why.”

      Ask for a source, counterexample, proof, reproduction or a test of applicability. Name what would settle the question.

      Bring something checkable.

      A person or agent supplies an artifact with its assumptions, inputs and limits. A useful failure belongs here too.

What this view does — and refuses to do

1 · A step is admitted only on evidence

A single-mutation step v → w is accepted only if the lower bound of w is at least the upper bound of v. The bounds come from the two measured replicates; the slider adds an assumed error bound on top. That inequality is exactly what the Lean checker is about — the compiled receipt, if any, appears in the Proof tab.

2 · Unmeasured is not impossible

Of 160,000 amino-acid genotypes, 871 have no measurement in the source table. Edges touching them stay unknown. A negative answer is reported as blocked only if the route is blocked even when every unmeasured genotype is allowed to be perfect; otherwise the answer is undetermined and the view names the assay to run.

3 · The answer is a decision, not a score

Open, blocked, or undetermined — with the weakest step, the assumption that would break it, and the evidence behind each row. Best-score optimisation is secondary. This panel is a descriptive full-data analysis; it is not a predictive benchmark.

Where this sits in the literature

Epistasis, fitness landscapes, indirect adaptive paths and machine-learning-guided directed evolution are all established. We claim none of them.

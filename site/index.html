What this view does — and refuses to do

1 · A step is admitted only on evidence

A single-mutation step v → w is accepted only if the lower bound of w is at least the upper bound of v. The bounds come from the two measured replicates; the slider adds an assumed error bound on top. That inequality is exactly what the Lean checker is about — the compiled receipt, if any, appears in the Proof tab.

2 · Unmeasured is not impossible

Of 160,000 amino-acid genotypes, 871 have no measurement in the source table. Edges touching them stay unknown. A negative answer is reported as no route certifiable only if that holds even when every unmeasured genotype is allowed to be perfect; otherwise the answer is undetermined and the view names the assay to run. “No route certifiable” is not “no ascending route exists”: overlapping intervals are simply not admitted.

3 · The answer is a decision, not a score

Open, no route certifiable, or undetermined — with the weakest step, the assumption that would break it, and the evidence behind each row. Best-score optimisation is secondary. This panel is a descriptive full-data analysis; it is not a predictive benchmark.

Does asking the right assay save experiments? retrospective replay, not a prospective result

Each policy starts from the same 96 observed genotypes plus the start and target, then pays an oracle for every further measurement. A certificate is issued only from revealed labels; runs without one by the budget stay in the denominator. The single-policy baselines are this repository's own, not ALDE.

When a claim becomes a calculation

Delphi already contains mathematical claims. We bind two of them to explicit model assumptions: the right context permits a calculation; the wrong context, a changed source, or a withdrawn claim causes a refusal.

Kimura: calculate or refuse

A declared diploid population can use the fixation model. An enzyme growth assay in haploid E. coli cannot silently become a selection coefficient.

Fisher: keep the scope

The selection component of change is not a promise that total fitness rises at every mutation. The adapter rejects that transfer.

Reuse the evidence

Same-context reuse matches ordinary caching. Typed records additionally catch deliberately injected context errors; those are software tests, not wet-lab savings.

A model prior, with its limits visible

NVIDIA BioNeMo ESM2-650M scored all 160,000 variants on a Nebius H100. Its zero-shot scores correlate weakly with measured growth (Spearman 0.17–0.18). A random-split check found almost no added predictive value beyond amino-acid identity.

These are predictions, not new measurements. The completed GPU sweep does not establish an assay-policy improvement. The OpenResearch integration ran the companion analysis locally on CPU. Read the runs, comparisons and limitations ↗

Before simulating a pattern, check the domain

The same declared reaction and diffusion parameters can admit an unstable mode on one domain and none on another. An existing Delphi Turing claim now drives a source-pinned check of those assumptions.

Explore the domain-length example ↗ This is linear mathematics for a declared model. Lean checks its algebra and mode bounds; numerical eigenvalues provide a separate cross-check. No cells, tissue or fabricated material were measured.

Where this sits in the literature

Epistasis, fitness landscapes, indirect adaptive paths and machine-learning-guided directed evolution are all established. We claim none of them.

Lean (integer arithmetic, no Mathlib)

Dispersion identities for any integer J, Du, Dv, domain p/s; positivity of det and negativity of trace for every mode beyond a cutoff (neumann_tail_hurwitz); this instance: all modes stable at L=π, exactly mode 1 unstable at L=2π (long_unstable_iff); det factorization (5q−1)(2q−1).

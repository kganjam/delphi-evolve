Challenge the assumption and the route moves: 5 steps → 6 → 7, then no route can be certified.

bold row: assumed κ · grey row: variants reachable from VFVS by certified steps

κ multiplies the replicate-disagreement spread to widen every measurement into an interval. It is an assumed sensitivity — not a confidence level. A step is certified only if the next variant’s lower bound clears the current one’s upper bound.

The weakest step of the κ=2 route

SFMS → SIMS clears the bar by only 0.0013 growth units; the route is certifiable up to κ≈2.45 and must be re-planned beyond.

Click a step in the live tool and the raw replicate rows and read counts for that variant appear.

Precisely: at κ=6 the tool says “no route certifiable at this bound” (25 variants reachable by certified steps). That does not mean no ascending route exists: overlapping intervals are simply not certified. κ=4 is the last bound in the sweep with a certified route.

When the evidence cannot settle a step, the system’s output is the next assay to run — not a confidence number it cannot support.

The 6-step route is checked by the Lean kernel — conditional on the intervals we assumed.

What the theorem says

If every true value lies inside its interval, and each step satisfies lower(next) ≥ upper(current), then the route never goes down.

A second theorem: a closed set whose every exit is certified lower rules out any non-decreasing path. Both are generic over any graph.

The wedge: a decision layer that says which assay, and why it is justified — tested next on a real enzyme campaign.

Buyer (hypothesis)

Economic buyer: head of protein engineering at an industrial-enzyme or biocatalysis group, or a CRO running variant campaigns.

Pays for: fewer wasted assays and an auditable reason for each one, above the protein models they already use.

Today: a retrospective tool on one public landscape. Future, unproven: a wet-lab cost or time advantage.

Next real experiment: one-lab prospective pilot

Lab supplies a prior campaign and a new target; we pre-register the decision rule, then both arms spend the same assay budget.

# Delphi: from candidate laws to checked theorems

The textbook inequality is green. This post describes a small executable check that asks whether it applies to the model in front of you, what it returns when it does not, what is compiled behind it, what is still pending, and the claims we held that did not survive.

## What we are not claiming

The conditions we check are known to experts, and we do not present them as discoveries. We ran no wet-lab experiment; the simulations are declared-model only. We do not say that any named law is wrong: a scope condition we supply refers to the extracted record of the claim. We did not build the first library of formal biology. ATLAS (facebookresearch/atlas-lean, MathlibExt/Biology) already holds a large body of exact biology statements with cited sources and non-vacuity witnesses, and Prove2Me (arXiv 2608.28433), Rashid et al. 2017, lean-bio, crnt-lean and Ripple (arXiv 2607.13531) came before at their own scope. We aim to complement ATLAS, not compete with it.

## The reveal

Take a declared two-species linear model, J = [[1, -2], [2, -3]] with diffusivities (1, 10) and Neumann boundaries. The continuum Turing condition holds: det(J − qD) = (5q-1)(2q-1) is negative for 1/5 < q < 1/2. On a finite domain only q = (nπ/L)² exist. At L = π none lies in the band; at 3π/2 and 2π one does; at 5π/2 none does again. Lean 4.35.0 compiles the L = π and L = 2π cases and rejects 3 mutants; the other lengths are exact rational arithmetic. The algorithm is trivial, counting admissible modes in a band, so what we offer is a certificate and packaging, not new mathematics. Non-monotone dependence on size is textbook-derivable and not new. This is a linear-mode statement; whether a pattern forms is a nonlinear question we have not settled (pending).

## The check and the repair

We gave the checker 16 requests for a target wavelength with equal diffusivities, which the plain formula accepts. It refused all 16 with a witness. The exact check then licensed a repair, raising the diffusivity ratio to 9 for most requests, and an independent solver found a pattern on 11 of 16 requests, mean success 0.708 (fraction of 3 noise seeds, averaged over requests). The plain formula's design without repair succeeded on 0, which is true by construction for equal diffusivities. The caveats matter: one kinetics, one-dimensional Neumann boundaries, 3 noise seeds, no confidence interval, near-onset repaired patterns that are marginal, five requests that did not succeed on all seeds, and no domain-length repair found. It is a model-only check, not an experiment.

## Compiled nodes and a test set

We compiled a conditional Hamilton regression theorem that depends on a selection-only lemma, with exact witnesses for each assumption and 2 rejected changed-sign mutants (Lean exit 0, standard axioms only). The Price identity underneath it is already formalized in ATLAS, so our version of that part is a duplicate; a critic's code search found no Hamilton form there, though the search is not exhaustive. A second reader has not yet reviewed the statements (pending). The test set has 127 requests in 8 families whose answers come from stated methods under declared conventions (exact rational arithmetic for Turing, Hamilton and Hawk–Dove items; numerical or closed-form for Kimura, Greenhill and Fisher–KPP); it is frozen and Codex's metadata and wording check passes, which does not audit the answers. On the 50 Turing, 2-D Turing and wavelength items the gate agrees with the key on 50, the plain formula on 29 and the continuum condition alone on 34. The set is trap-enriched and shares its convention document with the checker, so this is not external validation.

## The ledger

We kept a ledger of claims we held that did not survive: 21 rows, each with a witness and a kind. 16 were caught by an independent reviewer or validator, 2 by the author with an independent hold, and 3 only by the author's own measurement or rule. Kinds are kept separate: prior art 7, counterexample 1, measured 4, mis-operationalised 3, convention defect 4, process defect 1, design defect 1. There is no control arm, so this compares independent audit with self-audit; it is not a measurement of collective intelligence, and one team's day is not a rate for the field. One row is our own theorem, finite_domain_stable, whose premise (determinant non-negativity) was advertised as stability; the premise also holds for unstable systems. A revised module now compiles a stronger statement, with the determinant assumed and no new mutants yet.

## What the collective measured

On the controlled enzyme replay, shared specialists matched a single planner (78 of 100 each) and a plain lazy search reached 96, mostly in one repeated pair, with an assay-count interval that includes zero. In a proof-search pilot on 19 frozen integer-arithmetic lemmas every arm certified all 19 in every run, a ceiling; library reuse was real but saved no calls or tokens (median calls 21, 22, 20, 21). The statements are arithmetic surrogates, the arms are call-matched and not compute-matched, and the checker was repaired after the outcomes were seen. On the frozen test set, unguarded models without a code tool scored 121 and 110 of 127; the checker-tool arm was not run, the effective n is 8 families, and the check measures judgement rather than derivation. Two small comparisons showed no advantage for a shared-evidence collective, one of them at a ceiling that could not discriminate; nothing here says that agents need the gate, and the headroom result mostly shows the test set is not hard for a frontier model without tools.

## Infrastructure used

Receipts only. Five BioNeMo ESM2 sizes scored all 160,000 enzyme variants on Nebius H100s (10 jobs, all completed, 9,475 recorded job-seconds) in the released baseline, with no clear assay-saving benefit. Two OpenFold2 structure calls went to the Nebius hosted catalogue and came back predicted and low confidence. OpenResearch logged two local CPU analyses, one of the same data and one of the nonlinear sweep, neither hosted compute; no GPU or Nebius was used for the sweep. Gemini made 4 bounded review calls and was a subject of the headroom check. The Delphi checks themselves ran on CPU.

## Reusable now, and next

The checker, repair enumerator, test set and ledger are in the repository; outside use is none observed yet. Next, as a proposal and not a deployed system: a pilot on a modelling team's recent reuse decisions, and Delphi Frontier, one page per open problem showing what we know, what is next and who is working on it; our own seed questions failed our own validator, 0 of 7 bounded (ledger R21, single-reader, not re-run by the critic). A first scorecard of the 21 extracted records has 6 linked to scope-check benchmark families, 1 related formal lemma whose target is unestablished, 2 empirical or framework, and 12 without a corresponding check; the statuses are one reader's, revised after a Codex review of version 0, and the revision was not re-reviewed. A misprediction map over a large parameter ensemble is pending.

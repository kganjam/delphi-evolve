Challenge the assumption and the route moves: 5 steps → 6 → 7, then no route can be certified.

bold row: assumed κ · grey row: variants reachable from VFVS by certified steps

κ multiplies the replicate-disagreement spread to widen every measurement into an interval. It is an assumed sensitivity — not a confidence level. A step is certified only if the next variant’s lower bound clears the current one’s upper bound.

The weakest step of the κ=2 route

SFMS → SIMS clears the bar by only 0.0013 growth units; the route is certifiable up to κ≈2.45 and must be re-planned beyond.

Variants reachable from VFVS by certified steps: 577 at κ=0 → 137 at κ=2 → 25 at κ=6. In the live tool, clicking a step shows its raw replicate rows.

Precisely: at κ=6 the tool says “no route certifiable at this bound” (25 variants reachable by certified steps). That does not mean no ascending route exists: overlapping intervals are simply not certified. κ=4 is the last bound in the sweep with a certified route.

When the evidence cannot settle a step, the system’s output is the next assay to run — not a confidence number it cannot support.

Same context = ordinary cache. Of 488,601 recorded paid requests, 95.3% repeat an earlier one: typed reuse and a plain cache avoid exactly the same 465,564. Typed reuse gains nothing there.

Fault tests are not savings. In synthetic fixtures (4 injected fault types) a context-blind cache reused 2,564 invalid facts; typed reuse 0 and re-assayed them.

Bounded knowledge execution, not theorem extraction. Two existing claims, hand-authored adapters, recorded demo — not a live service. Checked — conditionally

Lean 4.35.0 compiled the generic route-soundness theorem, a weak “no certifiable route” cut and a strong cut; plus one 7-node VFVS → AIKG route recomputed exactly from raw CSV intervals (7/7); swapped-route mutant rejected (exit 1).

ASSUMED interval containment. Noise bins were fit on all replicates, so κ is a transductive sensitivity, not held-out validation. Not every one of the 1,400 replay-episode certificates (core arms plus LazySP) is kernel-checked.

Predicted — not measured

Five BioNeMo ESM2 sizes, 8M–3B, each scored all 160,000 variants on Nebius H100s. Weak rank association did not improve with size. OpenResearch ran a companion analysis locally on CPU.

Replay using this prior: 1,100 follow-up episodes: no clear assay-saving benefit; all descriptive RMST intervals include zero. Nothing here says it helps.

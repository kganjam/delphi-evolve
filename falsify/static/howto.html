Can your pattern actually form there?

Send a two-species reaction-diffusion linearization and a domain. We try to break the claim "a Turing-type instability exists on this domain" with exact rational arithmetic, and return a verdict, the complete list of unstable modes, the smallest domain that admits any, independent cross-checks, and a receipt you can re-run. Optional: a Lean 4 machine-checked proof of the classification.

Scope. A growing linear eigenmode is a prediction of linear instability under the assumptions you declare. It is not evidence of nonlinear pattern formation, of any biology, or of any wet-lab or clinical outcome. A "refuted" verdict means no admissible linear mode grows on that domain for those numbers, not that your system cannot pattern by other routes (growth, nonlinearity, noise, other boundaries).

curl -s {{BASE}}/v1/falsify/turing -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{

That example passes the textbook condition yet the answer on length π is domain_blocks_pattern (outcome refuted); change length_over_pi to "2" and mode 1 goes unstable (survived).

Your own units, or a standard model

{"caller":"your-team-name","model":{"name":"brusselator","params":{"A":1,"B":"3/2"}},
 "diffusivities":[1,30],"domain":{"dimension":1,"boundary":"dirichlet","length":12.5}}

Presets: brusselator (A,B), schnakenberg (a,b), gierer_meinhardt (a,b); fixed point verified exactly. Otherwise give the Jacobian at your homogeneous steady state. Numbers may be ints, decimals or exact strings like "3/2". Physical length uses a rigorous rational bracket of π. 2-D rectangles: "dimension":2, "lengths_over_pi":[3,2]. Boundaries: neumann, dirichlet, periodic.

How much faster must the inhibitor diffuse, for your kinetics?

Every response includes diffusion_requirement: for your Jacobian, the exact bracket of the inhibitor/activator diffusivity ratio the textbook band needs (exactly 9 for the example above), or "no ratio suffices". There is no universal threshold. The required ratio is a property of the kinetics: it always exceeds |δ|/α (>1) and approaches it only for near-singular, finely tuned kinetics, so a ratio of 1.1 can suffice (Lean-checked example in the test suite). Figures like "7 to 10" are rules of thumb for particular kinetics. The optional proteins field is a crude mass-scaling heuristic (D ∝ M-1/3), not a validated structure-to-diffusion calculation: radius of gyration is not hydrodynamic radius, and effective tissue transport (binding, hindrance, dimerization) is not modelled.

Verdicts

  • survived / pattern_possible: ≥1 admissible mode has det(J−qD)<0 (list returned is complete).
  • refuted / domain_blocks_pattern: the textbook band is non-empty but no admissible mode lies in it.
  • refuted / no_turing_instability: no band at all.
  • out_of_scope: not a 2-species system with a stable well-mixed state, unsupported boundary, etc. (reason given).
  • unresolvable: marginal mode or bracket too coarse; we say so rather than guess.
